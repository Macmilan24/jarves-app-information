Jarves helps an invited manager track delegated work, review email follow-ups and manage reminders through a private Telegram conversation.

What Jarves does

  • Reviews connected email for requests, assignments, completion reports and deadlines.
  • Keeps work records visible in a Google Sheets tracker and a dedicated Google Tasks list.
  • Maintains a separate reminder calendar and delivers scheduled Telegram notices.
  • Accepts text and English voice notes, including work discussed outside email.
  • Provides morning priorities and end-of-day reports from recorded work.

Connecting Google

The invited user opens Jarves in Telegram, chooses Connect Google and reviews Google's permission page. Jarves checks the connected account against the invitation before preparing the tracker, task list and reminder calendar.

Gmail access is read-only. Jarves can identify email follow-ups and prepare reply text in conversation; it does not send email. The connected user can revoke Google access at any time.

Google may display an unverified-app warning for this privately distributed app. Review the requested permissions and contact the operator if you are unsure about connecting.

Data and support

Connected information is processed to provide the assistant's features. Relevant content may be sent to a configured AI provider when hosted processing is enabled. The privacy notice explains the information involved and the connection controls.

Terms of use

Effective 20 October 2026

Invited access

Jarves is privately provided to invited users for operational administration. Connect only accounts and information you are authorized to use. Access may also depend on your organization's Google Workspace settings and its policies for connected services.

Assistant actions

Jarves uses your requests and connected records to help manage operational work. Its current Gmail connection is read-only. A recorded assignment does not itself send instructions to a staff member. Staff completion reports remain separate from manager verification.

AI-generated interpretations can be incomplete or incorrect. Review important dates, assignments and conclusions against their linked records. The responsible manager remains in control of operational decisions.

Availability and connected services

Operation depends on the configured hosting, model allowance, Google connection and Telegram service. Access can be interrupted by outages, exhausted model budgets, account changes or revoked permissions. Check connection notices and source records before relying on an automated update.

Privacy and ending access

The privacy notice describes information handled by the assistant. You can stop using Jarves and revoke its Google access through your Google account. Contact support for disconnection or deletion of retained assistant records.
